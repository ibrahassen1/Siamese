# Siamese — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

Siamese is a stock market simulator that gives users virtual money to trade stocks using live or near-real-time market data. Users can use the app to practice investing without risking real money.

Eventually, Siamese will also have fantasy-style leagues where users can compete against their friends based on portfolio performance.

## 2. Problem Statement

Getting into stocks can be intimidating, especially when real money is involved. Siamese gives users a simple and beginner-friendly way to get hands-on experience with the stock market without having to deal with financial risk.

## 3. Target User

Our main target user is someone who wants to get into stocks and gain experience trading without risking their own money.

## 4. V1 Requirements

For V1, users need to be able to:

* Create an account and log in
* Start with virtual money
* Search for stocks
* View current and historical stock data
* Buy and sell stocks
* View their current holdings
* View their cash, investments, net worth, and returns
* Track their portfolio performance
* View their transaction history
* Download their transaction history as a CSV
* Manage basic account settings

## 5. Out of Scope

The following will not be included in V1:

* Fantasy leagues
* Friends and social features
* Leaderboards
* Real-money trading
* WebSocket market streaming
* Options, margin, or short selling
* Advanced trading tools

These can be added after the Practice Portfolio is working correctly.

## 6. Success Criteria

V1 is complete when a user can create an account, receive virtual money, search for a stock, buy and sell shares, and accurately track their cash, holdings, transactions, and overall portfolio performance from beginning to end.
